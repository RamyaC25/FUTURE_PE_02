# FUTURE_PE_02 – AI UGC Ad Generator for Local Business

## Task 2 – Prompt Engineering Internship

### Business Information

**Business Name:** Glow Beauty Salon  
**Business Type:** Beauty Salon  
**Location:** Shivamogga, Karnataka  
**Service:** Hair Spa

### Project Objective

The objective of this project is to use structured AI prompts to generate engaging UGC-style social media advertisements for a local beauty salon.

The content is designed for short-form social media platforms such as Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.

### Target Audience

Women and girls in Shivamogga interested in:

- Hair care
- Beauty and grooming
- Self-care
- Salon services

### Deliverables

The project includes:

- Multiple UGC hooks
- Problem → Solution → CTA ad variations
- Instagram Reels adaptation
- Facebook Reels adaptation
- YouTube Shorts adaptation
- Friendly & Relatable tone
- Premium & Elegant tone
- Young & Playful tone
- On-screen text suggestions
- Suggested captions
- Clear calls-to-action
- Prompt logic
- AI workflow
- Key learnings
- Conclusion

### Prompting Approach

The prompts were structured using:

1. Business information
2. Target audience
3. Service information
4. Platform requirements
5. Tone and style
6. Hook requirements
7. Problem → Solution → CTA structure
8. On-screen text
9. Caption requirements
10. Quality constraints

### Quality Constraints

The prompts instructed the AI to:

- Keep the content realistic and relatable.
- Avoid fake claims and guaranteed results.
- Avoid unsupported statistics.
- Avoid invented prices, offers and reviews.
- Keep the content suitable for social media advertising.
- Adapt the language and style to the target audience.

### AI Workflow

Business Information  
↓  
Target Audience  
↓  
Service Information  
↓  
Platform & Tone  
↓  
Structured Prompt  
↓  
AI Generated UGC Content  
↓  
Platform Adaptation  
↓  
Review & Refinement  
↓  
Final Social Media Ad Content

### Key Learnings

1. Learned how to structure prompts for UGC-style advertising.
2. Learned how hooks can be used to capture audience attention.
3. Learned how to create Problem → Solution → CTA content.
4. Learned how the same business information can be adapted for different platforms.
5. Learned how tone changes the style of social media content.
6. Learned how AI can generate multiple creative variations from structured prompts.
7. Learned the importance of quality constraints when generating advertising content.

This project demonstrates how structured prompt engineering can be used to create social-media-ready UGC advertisements for a local business.

The workflow combines business information, audience targeting, platform adaptation, tone variation and quality constraints to generate useful and engaging promotional content.
