# TASK 2 – AI Content Marketing using UGC Ads

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

The objective of this project is to use structured AI prompts to create authentic, relatable and conversion-focused UGC-style advertising content for a local beauty business.

The project focuses on creating short-form UGC ad content for Glow Beauty Salon, Shivamogga, Karnataka, promoting its Hair Spa service.

## 2. BUSINESS INFORMATION

**Business Name:** Glow Beauty Salon

**Business Type:** Local Beauty Salon

**Location:** Shivamogga, Karnataka

**Service Promoted:** Hair Spa

## 3. TARGET AUDIENCE

Women and girls in Shivamogga who are interested in hair care, grooming and self-care.

## 4. CONTENT CREATED

- UGC-style advertisement script
- Multiple hooks for the same service
- Problem → Solution → CTA ad variations
- Instagram Reels adaptation
- Facebook Reels adaptation
- YouTube Shorts adaptation
- Friendly & Relatable brand tone
- Premium & Elegant brand tone
- Young & Playful brand tone

## 5. PROMPT 1 – UGC AD SCRIPT

### Prompt Used

You are an expert prompt engineer and UGC advertising copywriter.

Create a UGC-style short-form advertisement for a local beauty salon.

Business name: Glow Beauty Salon
Location: Shivamogga, Karnataka
Business type: Local Beauty Salon
Service being promoted: Hair Spa

Target audience:
Women and girls in Shivamogga who are interested in hair care, grooming and self-care.

Create one authentic, relatable UGC-style ad script for Instagram Reels.

Requirements:

1. Start with a relatable customer problem or situation.
2. Create a natural UGC-style hook in the first few seconds.
3. Introduce Glow Beauty Salon and its Hair Spa service naturally.
4. Explain the service without making medical claims or guaranteeing results.
5. Make the script sound like a real customer sharing an experience, not like a formal advertisement.
6. Use simple and conversational language.
7. Include a clear call-to-action encouraging viewers to enquire or book an appointment.
8. Keep the script suitable for a short-form video.
9. Do not invent prices, offers, ratings, statistics, customer reviews or specific results.
10. Make the content conversion-focused but authentic.

Format the answer as:

- Hook
- Problem
- Solution
- UGC Script
- CTA
- Suggested on-screen text

### UGC Ad Output

**Hook**

“Okay, am I the only one whose hair starts looking SO dull after a hectic week? 😭”

**Problem**

“Lately my hair was feeling really dry and just… not looking its best. I wanted to do something for my hair, but I didn’t want anything complicated.”

**Solution**

“So I decided to try a Hair Spa at Glow Beauty Salon here in Shivamogga.”

**UGC Script**

“Honestly, I just wanted some proper hair-care time for myself. At Glow Beauty Salon, I went in for their Hair Spa, and the whole experience felt like a nice little self-care break.

They take you through the hair-spa process and focus on caring for your hair and scalp. I really liked taking some time out from my usual routine and giving my hair some attention.

If you’re in Shivamogga and you’ve been thinking about getting a Hair Spa, you can definitely check out Glow Beauty Salon and see if it’s right for you.”

**CTA**

“Want to give your hair some self-care too? 💆‍♀️✨
DM Glow Beauty Salon or contact them to enquire and book your Hair Spa appointment.”

**Suggested On-screen Text**

- “Hair feeling dull lately? 😭”
- “Time for some hair self-care 💆‍♀️”
- “Hair Spa at Glow Beauty Salon ✨”
- “📍 Shivamogga, Karnataka”
- “Enquire & book your appointment 💕”

## 10. PROMPT LOGIC

The prompts were structured using the following elements:

### Business Information

The business name, business type, location and service were provided so that the AI could generate content specifically for the selected local business.

### Target Audience

The target audience was specified as women and girls in Shivamogga who are interested in hair care, grooming and self-care. This helped make the generated content relevant to the intended audience.

### Service

The Hair Spa service was clearly specified so that the AI could create focused and service-specific UGC advertising content.

### UGC Style

The prompts instructed the AI to create natural, conversational and relatable content that sounds like a real person sharing an experience rather than a formal advertisement.

### Platform Requirements

The prompts included requirements for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts so that the content could be adapted to different short-form platforms.

### Brand Tone

The prompts requested three different tones:

- Friendly & Relatable
- Premium & Elegant
- Young & Playful

This allowed the same service to be communicated in different brand styles.

### Conversion Requirements

The prompts included clear calls-to-action encouraging viewers to enquire, contact or book the Hair Spa service.

### Quality Constraints

The prompts instructed the AI to avoid fake prices, offers, reviews, ratings, statistics, medical claims and guaranteed results.
